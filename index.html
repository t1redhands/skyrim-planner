Perk plan at levels 20, 40 and 60

Choose the perks you want and the milestone you want each one by. You get one perk point per level, so level 20 allows 19 perks, level 40 allows 39 and level 60 allows 59. Each perk sets the skill level you need, and those skill levels set how far your character has leveled.

Health, Magicka and Stamina

Every level-up adds 10 to one attribute. Split the level-ups in each stretch of play; each point of Stamina also adds 5 carry weight.

Totals at each milestone

Standing Stones

Only one stone is active at a time, but you can switch whenever you reach another stone. Pick one for each stretch of play. The Warrior, Mage, Thief and Lover stones make skills improve faster, so they get you to each milestone sooner. The others give a lasting ability or a once-a-day power.
