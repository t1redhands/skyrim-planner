Perk plan at levels 20, 40 and 60

Choose the perks you want and the milestone you want each one by. You get one perk point per level, so level 20 allows 19 perks, level 40 allows 39 and level 60 allows 59. Each perk sets the skill level you need, and those skill levels set how far your character has leveled.

Health, Magicka and Stamina

Every level-up adds 10 to one attribute. Split the level-ups in each stretch of play; each point of Stamina also adds 5 carry weight.

Totals at each milestone

Standing Stones

Only one stone is active at a time, but you can switch whenever you reach another stone. Pick one for each stretch of play. The Warrior, Mage, Thief and Lover stones make skills improve faster, so they get you to each milestone sooner. The others give a lasting ability or a once-a-day power.

Shouts

Choose the shouts you want and the milestone you want each one by. Each shout has three words, found at word walls or given during quests, and each word takes one dragon soul to unlock. Some quests unlock their words for you.

Potions and poisons

Pick up to three effects and the planner finds the ingredient combinations that brew them, with the fewest unwanted side effects first. Save the ones you like as favorites for a milestone.

Food

Cooked food is made at a cooking pot or spit, baked food at a Hearthfire oven, and hot soups only matter in Survival mode, where they also warm you. Choose favorites and the milestone you want them by.

Scrolls

Say how much this character will lean on scrolls and spider scrolls. Both give magic without Magicka or magic skill, which suits warriors and thieves as well as mages.

      Homes

      Homes you can buy or build, and the rooms your factions give you. Homes with the crafting stations your perks use are marked. Any quest a home needs goes into your quest plan.

      Quest plan

      Your quests in three tiers: Expedite for what the build needs, Priority for role-play that suits the character, and Selected for everything else you want to do. Prerequisites are pulled in automatically and listed first. Tick quests off as you finish them.
