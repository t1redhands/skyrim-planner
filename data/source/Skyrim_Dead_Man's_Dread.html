Dead Man's Dread is a Creation that adds two pirate swords, new outfits, and a pirate ship player home.

Overview[edit]

This Creation adds the quest The Restless, which will lead you to a subterranean ship on an island in the Sea of Ghosts. Within this haunted wreck you will find relics of Cyrus, the protagonist of Redguard. After you have cleared the island of its undead inhabitants, you can claim the ship as your player home. It comes fully furnished with crafting stations, a mannequin, and plenty of storage.

This Creation also adds the Blackwater Blade, an artifact that previously appeared in Oblivion.
