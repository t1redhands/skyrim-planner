Nirnroot is a bright green plant normally found growing near water. Nirnroot plants glow, making them easier to locate at night, and also emit a unique chiming sound. While in-game text claims Nirnroot does not regrow, it actually does regrow normally like other plants (unlike its behavior in Oblivion). A related plant is Crimson Nirnroot, which has nearly identical alchemical properties, with only certain differences in the Damage Health and Damage Stamina effects. Nirnroot and Crimson Nirnroot can be combined to make a four-effect potion, as shown below.

Damage Health poisons made using Nirnroot have the same strength as those made using standard Damage Health ingredients. However, the gold value of such poisons is 12.6 times greater than those made using standard ingredients. See the effect page for details.

Notes[edit]

  • Nirnroot can be sold to Avrusa Sarethi at Sarethi Farm for the quest Gather Wheat for 10 gold each.
  • Nirnroot and Crimson Nirnroot are not affected by the Alchemy perk Green Thumb; therefore, even with the perk, you will still only collect a single sample from each plant. However, with the Dragonborn add-on installed, several regular Nirnroot plants can be found in Solstheim which yield multiple samples of the ingredient.
  • With the Hearthfire add-on, you cannot plant Nirnroot at the greenhouse or garden of your player-built property.
  • Although Gianna claims during the quest To Kill an Empire that Nirnroot can be added to soups as "a special seasoning", you cannot do this yourself when cooking.
