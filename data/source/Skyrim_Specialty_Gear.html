This page summarizes all of the Specialty Gear in Skyrim. These are items that can be found in many locations in the game, yet do not qualify as generic weapons or generic armor. For example, it includes weapons and armor that are only used by specific races or factions (e.g., the Stormcloaks, the Thieves Guild, the Blades). It also includes some items, such as pickaxes and woodcutter's axes, that are only used in certain situations.

Silver Hand Equipment[edit]

Name (ID) Type Tempering Notes
Silver Sword
(0010aa19)		 One-Handed Sword possible 7 100 8

Especially effective against undead.

  • Silver weapons can be found wielded by Silver Hand members during the Companions questline.
    • A Silver Sword can also be found in Valerica's Study.
  • Silver weapons add +20 to their base damage when attacking werewolves, werebearsDB, and undead, including some ghosts (ghostly animals are still animals, and some humanoid ghosts are still marked as their base race instead) and vampires. Your character's weapon skill does not affect this hidden enchantment. The Barbarian and Armsman perks, however, do affect it.
  • This effect is not considered an enchantment, so silver weapons can be enchanted like any normal unenchanted weapon, though they will always display the text "Especially effective against undead."
    • The Unofficial Skyrim Patch, version 1.0, addresses this issue. Enchantment text is made visible but for this to work the notice about undead had to be removed, and information is now provided via a loading screen text entry.
    • PC players can use the mod Enchantable Special Item Fix to instead properly concatenate the text so they can see both the intrinsic special effect and the added enchantments.
  • Using a spell (healing, oak flesh) while holding a silver weapon negates the extra damage, re-equipping the weapon fixes this. Tested on Special Edition. ?
  • Sometimes silver weapons do not show any description text.
  • Silver weapons cannot be improved at a grindstone.
    • The Unofficial Skyrim Patch, version 1.0, addresses this issue. Tempering is made possible with a silver ingot.
