Unique Weapons are weapons available in Skyrim generally found in fixed locations, unlike most equipment in the game, which are randomly generated or generic equipment (see Generic Magic Weapons). Some of these weapons can be improved through smithing. Improving enchanted weapons requires the Arcane Blacksmith perk.

Many of these items are quest rewards, although some of the most valuable quest rewards are listed at Leveled Items (unique items, but with level-dependent statistics) and Artifacts (unusually powerful unique items with unique appearances and enchantments). For unique items that are needed to complete quests, please see the Quest Items article. For other unique items in the game, see the Unique Items article. For a list of the other available weapons, see the weapon category.

Bloodcursed Elven Arrow[edit]

A Bloodcursed Elven Arrow is an elven arrow that has been tainted with the blood of a Daughter of Coldharbour, a female vampire created by Molag Bal himself. The arrows are visually similar to the elven arrows that are needed to create them, with a gray shaft instead of a golden one, with the fletchings being alternating shades of gray. The quiver is similar, being a more bleak color compared to the strong golden one of the normal arrows. When fired at the sun during the day with Auriel's Bow, it will block out its light for the rest of the day, negating the damage vampires suffer from it. It is the counterpart of the Sunhallowed Elven Arrow.

For more information, see the Bloodcursed Elven Arrow article.

Sunhallowed Elven Arrow[edit]

Unique Item: Sunhallowed Elven Arrow (xx0098a1)
Type Arrow
Added by Dawnguard
Editor ID DLC1ElvenArrowBlessed
Statistics
 Damage 16
 Damage 16
 Weight 0  Value 16
Enchantment
Causes sunburst attacks to nearby targets if shot at the sun with Auriel's Bow
A quiver of Sunhallowed Elven Arrows

A Sunhallowed Elven Arrow is an elven arrow that has been blessed by Knight-Paladin Gelebor. The arrows are visually similar to the elven arrows that are needed to create them, with a gray shaft instead of a golden one, and the fletchings alternating between gray and white. The quiver is similar, being a more pallid color compared to the strong golden one of normal arrows. The arrowhead, however, is a bright, glowing gold; not the standard gold of regular elven arrows. When fired at the sun during the day with Auriel's Bow, it will cause a sunburst, doing damage to any target caught in the blast, with undead taking triple damage. It is the counterpart of the Bloodcursed Elven Arrow.

When used against enemies with Auriel's Bow, they cause small explosions similar to a sunburst attack. The effect isn't dependent on the bow's enchantment, so it can be used even if the enchantment is empty, though the arrows are destroyed by the attack, so it isn't possible to retrieve the arrows from bodies or missed shots in the environment. If you use the arrows with other bows, there are no special effects and they behave like regular arrows. This is the same if you give sunhallowed arrows to followers.

For more information, see the Sunhallowed Elven Arrow article.

Horksbane

Horksbane is a steel mace found next to the corpse of Saden, its former owner, on Horker Island. It is identical to a normal steel mace in all respects. Horksbane was meant to deal 20 more damage to horkers, but doesn't due to a bug. Despite its effect, it can be enchanted further. Horksbane can be tempered with a steel ingot, with the improvement from the tempering being doubled if the Steel Smithing perk is also unlocked.

Saden's Journal sheds some light on how Horksbane was created. In life, Saden was a member of the Blood Horkers pirate gang. Their leader, Haldyn, ordered Saden and some other pirates to scout out islands that could be used for an outpost. When the arrived on Horker Island, several of Saden's crew members were set upon by the horkers living there and killed. The sight of his crew members being slaughtered made Saden so angry that his anger coursed into the steel mace he was holding, creating Horksbane.
