Telekinesis Arrow
(FExxx800)		 0 8 111 1 2 Void Salts
Makes 10 arrows 		Remains suspended in the air until released using the Lesser Power "Telekinesis Arrows".
  • Controlled via the Telekinesis Arrows Lesser Power
  • Can be crafted upon picking up Telekinesis Arrows Research Notes in the Arcanaeum
  • These arrows originally had a damage value of 10 before an update for Arcane Archer Pack was released in December 2018. The arrows were changed from a damage value of 30 to 111 in the Anniversary Edition Update.
  • Some NPCS may be equipped with Telekinesis Arrow that the player can't obtain, It can be fired as a regular arrow.
  • On PS5 Anniversary Edition, giving a playable Telekinesis Arrow to an NPC who practices archery allows the NPC to repeatedly fire it. The arrows remain suspended in mid-air and can be picked up by the player. Once ten arrows are suspended, further arrows may be returned directly to the player’s inventory, effectively allowing the arrows to be duplicated.
